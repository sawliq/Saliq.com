(01) — Overview

The short version

TL;DR

Exzyt is a gated M&A marketplace for the UAE. Sellers list verified businesses. Buyers browse by sector, sign an NDA to see the numbers, and move from NDA to LOI to SPA in one place, with every document's status in view.

As founding designer I owned the product end to end: research, information architecture, the public website, the buyer and seller portals, the brand system and the engineering handoff.

(02) — The problem

Two sides, one inbox

Sellers · Buyers · Business

Business acquisitions in the UAE ran on email chains and broker phone calls.

Sellers

They couldn't list without exposing their numbers.

— Financials reached unqualified buyers too early.

— Revenue data, trade licenses and audits went out as email attachments.

— 60% of their time went to filtering out non-serious buyers.

Buyers

They couldn't find, compare or track deals.

— No central place to browse by industry or price range.

— Discovery depended on brokers and word of mouth.

— NDAs, LOIs and SPAs were scattered across email with no status.

The business problem

Deals took months longer than they needed to.

Sellers were flooded with weak interest, buyers chased poor-fit businesses, and legal paperwork moved through email and PDFs. The goal: undercut market fees and take the selling fee to zero.

How might we

Let sellers be discovered without being exposed, and let buyers move from interest to signed deal in one place?

(04) — Strategy

Four calls that shaped it

Highlights · Constraints

  1. 01

    Dual registration over one sign-up

    Separate seller and buyer onboarding matches each real entry point.

  2. 02

    The NDA is the gate

    Details stay hidden until an NDA is signed, which answers sellers' fear of early exposure.

  3. 03

    KYC before any listing

    Every listing comes from a verified entity. Short-term friction, long-term trust.

  4. 04

    Browse by category, not search-first

    Buyers explore by sector, matching how they actually shop for a business.

Constraints we designed within

  • Trust vs. transparency

    Show enough to attract buyers without exposing the business. Progressive disclosure.

  • Legal documents, consumer feel

    NDA, LOI and SPA had to feel simple without losing legal weight.

  • Two user types, one product

    Buyers and sellers need different journeys inside one platform.

  • KYC is mandatory

    Verification is a hard blocker in UAE M&A, so it can't be deferred.

(06) — Deep dive

5 product decisions

Problem · Decision · Why it worked

01

NDA-gating business details

Problem

Show data publicly, gate everything, or find a middle path?

Decision

Progressive disclosure. Revenue range, industry, asking price and growth are public. Full financials, licenses and audits need a signed NDA.

Why it worked

It answers sellers' fear of premature exposure while still giving buyers enough to qualify a deal.

Decision board · 01

Replace with Figma export

02

KYC blocks listing creation

Problem

Faster sign-up to listing, or verified listings only?

Decision

Sellers get into their dashboard straight away, but creating a listing redirects to KYC first.

Why it worked

Every listing is tied to a verified identity, without blocking first-time exploration.

Decision board · 02

Replace with Figma export

03

One pattern for NDA → LOI → SPA

Problem

Each document carries different legal weight. One UI may oversimplify; three custom UIs mean three learning curves.

Decision

A single Document Progress component with status badges, reused for all three documents.

Why it worked

Users stay oriented through the whole deal, and it saved weeks of engineering effort.

(07) — Edge cases

Designed for when it breaks

6 covered · 4 known gaps

Designed and covered
  • KYC states: pending, approved, failed with re-upload
  • Upload errors for file type and size, shown inline
  • Empty states for listings, NDA, LOI and SPA
  • Typed confirmation before deleting a listing
  • A required reason when rejecting an NDA or LOI
  • Chat states: text, image, document, read receipts
Known gaps
  • No offline handling
  • No notifications for incoming NDAs, LOIs or messages
  • No error state for a failed e-signature
  • No bulk actions for sellers

(10) — Risks & trade-offs

What could go wrong

  • Risk

    Over-gating kills discovery

    NDA friction may put off early browsers. Range-based financial summaries before the gate could help.

  • Risk

    KYC bottleneck

    Manual verification delays could slow seller activation.

  • Risk

    Platform dependency

    An outage would stall active deals worth potentially millions.

  • Risk

    Single-language launch

    English-only limits reach, and the layouts don't support RTL yet.

(11) — What stays human

Where people stay in the loop

  • Human

    KYC review

    A person confirms the business is legitimate and seller-owned.

  • Human

    NDA, LOI and SPA acceptance

    Every document needs an explicit human action. Nothing is auto-accepted.

  • Human

    Deal room conversations

    No chatbots and no auto-replies.

  • Human

    Listing moderation

    Editors check that sensitive details aren't published.
